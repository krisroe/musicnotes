How your House is looked at (Internet meme image) (Stephen Ratzlaff campaign version)

...by you: [picture of a nice two car garage house, possibly egotistically a bit nicer than reality]
...the buyer: [picture of a one car garage house, making it worse than it actually is to corresponding with a lower price]
...the Bank loan: [picture of a small shack, allows charging a higher interest rate, limits the amount willing to loan to prevent overexposure where the loan exceeds the house's worth]
...the Insurance Evaluator: [picture of a tent with a thunderstorm coming, enhances the possibility of disasters to allow charging more for homeowner's insurance, reducing the risk for the Insurance company]
...by the County Appraisal District [picture of a mansion, justifying high property taxes. This is also the largest picture, suggesting the government's mismatch is the larges]

Note: This meme was found in a candidate for Dane County Executive (Wisconsin). The campaign has unserious elements, with singular focusi on property taxes. The meme is part of this focus. From Internet searching, the source for this version of the meme says "...by the Municipality for rates and taxes" instead of "...by the County Appraisal Dictrict" (the font change makes it obvious it was changed from the original material). The weird part of the change is a "County Appraisal District" is a Texas thing, not a Wisconsin thing (Wisconsin uses the more common method of municipality based tax rates). It's weird having a Wisconsin candidate mischaracterize Wisconsin property tax procedures.
