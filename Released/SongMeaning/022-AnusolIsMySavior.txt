Anusol is my Savior (Original Song)

Anusol!
Anusol!
Anusol!
You are my savior!

Meaning: This song was created when my spouse was pregnant. Spouse had hemorrhoids and had to use a cream to deal with them. In response I created this song. The tune and "savior" lyric are intentionally made to sound as if it is sacred. But it's real secular purpose is it points out an unpleasant reality of having hemorrhoids. Spouse didn't like this song, both because it reminded her of the hemorrhoids, and the religious-like nature of the song conflicted with her Episcopalian religion. In total, that makes the song into more of an insult song than anything.
