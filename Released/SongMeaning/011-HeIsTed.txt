Meaning: Ted (shortening of the longer name Teddy) was a pet dog we had growing up. When he heard an unfamiliar noise, he gave a distinctive confused look. This led to my sister and I making his defining characteristic stupidity. This song is an insult song attempting to describe stupid in an over-the-top way. I as the singer claim to prefer a hamster as a pet (which wasn't really true because Ted's shortcomings in intelligence were comedy gold). Some of the observations were true, he was well-fed as a household pet although he was not obese. He was undoubtedly a product of inbreeding common in farm animals where his parentage came from, he didn't shed very much (he grew very shaggy and had to be shaved periodically). "No grey matter in his head" and "He's brain-dead" are beyond possible characterizations of stupidity (he did have a functional brain). He wasn't even as dumb as the song makes him out to be, as he was trainable to the point of showing at the county fair for 4-H dog training. But he was in reality a nervous dog, and that combined with his attachment to my mother prevented him from being truly effective as a dog training dog for show. And resentment over the fact that he couldn't be trained effectively for show is part of why this song was created.

I try to button up the song at the end by saying there is no more to be said. I'm also particularly proud of the ambiguity for the "he is" between "For there is no one as dumb as he is" and "He is Ted".
