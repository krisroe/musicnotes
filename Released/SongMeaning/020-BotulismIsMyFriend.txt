Botulism is my Friend (Insult Song)

Botulism is my friend.
Botulism is my friend.
Botulism is my friend.
Botulism is my friend.

Meaning:
My spouse tends toward germophobic, and historically has responded negatively to claims (even obviously false ones) that some canned foodstuff has botulism. I struggle with communication with my spouse sometimes, and I have claimed on more than one occasion she is not really my friend. This is in the vein of things that are true but aren't wise to point out (she's never responded to the claims). So the song is really an insult song, saying the disease of
botulism is more of a friend than she is.
