La Gorda (Original Song)

Meaning: This is an insult song to my Sister. "La gorda" refers to a fat female. "Se llama Sonia" identifies the target of the song as Sonia. "Rompe una silla" means she breaks a chair (sitting on it because she is fat). The song as originally sung was "Estupida (stupid) Hija (daughter)" but later was changed to "Hija estupida" since Spanish adjectives typically go after the object noun (it doesn't sound as good but for that one I wanted it to be grammatically defensible). The song is also an insult against my mother by saying my sister's mother drank a lot of tequila. This perhaps implied my sister's failings could have something to do with my mother's failings. I am not entirely sure the use of the verb tomar for drinking tequila is correct, but at this point the song is complete and I don't want to look it up (if it's wrong, it's wrong, and it's just an insult song).
