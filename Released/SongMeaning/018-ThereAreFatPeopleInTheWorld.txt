There are Fat People in the World (Original Song)

There are fat people in the world (cha cha cha).
One of them is named Sonya Rowe.
For she is fat and dumb, 
She's fat and she's a bum,
She is a dirty trum.
Ha ha ha ha ha ha ha ha ha (cha cha cha).
She gets sent to la la la la la.
Now I'll be on my way.
Please do have a nice day.
Remember what I say (one two three)
'Bout Sonya Rowe (cha cha cha).

Meaning: This is an insult song. It describes my sister as fat and unintelligence. It has callbacks to the Sonya's Dumb song, calling her
a bum and a dirty trum, and getting sent to la la la. The end of the song ends with politeness, making humor in the constrast between the 
final politeness and the base insults. The song politely tells the listener the song will end (be on my way), that the listener should have a nice day,
and asks the listener should remember what the song says. That contrasts with the song's expected effective, which is an impartial listener will
ignore the song.
