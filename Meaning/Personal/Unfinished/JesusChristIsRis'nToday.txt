The title character Jesus Christ is a central figure in Christianity. This is an Easter hymn that celebrates Jesus Christ's resurrection after his death on Good Friday. Easter is one of the holiest days in Christianity, with Christmas (Christ's birth) the only serious competitor.

There's a lot of back story here.

The first people (Adam and Eve) were made perfect in God's image. God made a rule that only perfect humans could live in the Garden of Eden. However, there was forbidden knowledge God did not trust humans with. That forbidden knowledge was placed in the Tree of Knowledge. When Adam and Eve ate from the fruit of the Tree of Knowledge, they gained some information about what made people perfect. It was a tiny fraction of the total in the Tree of Knowledge, but it was enough to change Adam and Eve's behavior to render them no longer perfect and thus no longer welcome in the Garden of Eden, and banished to the world we know today.

The Garden of Eden has since been rebranded as heaven, and God did a lot of work (including personal observation of human behavior) to update his rules about who can get into heaven (for example strict perfection is no longer required). But no one was actually making it into heaven, and God didn't understand what was happening.
