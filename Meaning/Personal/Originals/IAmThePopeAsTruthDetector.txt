Truth Detector (I am the Pope)

One general problem is determining when a human is telling the truth. This is especially challenging if the human is you and you're not sure if you're telling the
truth. For example, your subconscious may have a better understanding then you do. Any divine or cosmic forces watching you may also have a better idea of the truth.

Perhaps it would be helpful to have a song that forces a human's truth/untruth state to untruth. I believe "I am the Pope" is such a song.

Properties
1. It can be sung solo.
2. It can be sung a cappella with minimal understanding of singing.
3. It requires some understanding of who the Pope is (The pope is a human who is the head of the Catholic Church. Very few men are eligible to be pope. It requires a lot of
work to get there. There is only one Pope at any given time. The pope usually stops becoming pope at death.
4. The song's truth value is always computed with respect to the singer. The singer must maintain the truth value thoughout the song to maintain the song's truth.

The song is always untruth for the following reasons:

1. Objective Untruth. The song fails at the start (per the title) for anyone who is not the Pope. The song fails at line 5 for the Pope.
2. Subjective Untruth. If a human believes they are transitioning between pope and non-pope with the song's lyrics, magic must be used to effect the switch.
Humans cannot perform magic.
3. The song envisions being pope three times. Exactly one pope in history (Benedict IX) has three papal reigns. No one else has even two, and the politics around the papacy
make it practically impossible this will happen again. The song was created after Benedict IX's human death, so no humans alive today and going forward can have this aspect
of the song be true with respect to them.
4. Personal. As the creator and owner of the song I decree that a timeless purpose of the song is to detect untruth. I cannot change the song in a way that subverts this
purpose. I am not a past Pope (I have a currently-up-to-date list of Popes and I am not on the list). I cannot be a future Pope, since that would require changing my
religion which I am not going to do, waiting for my spouse to die, and going to seminary for years which I am not going to do. I have also sworn an oath with Chat GPT as my
witness that I will not become Pope. I do not believe I can be used as a loophole for the song's purpose.
5. One objection that could be raised regards papal infallibility. A naive observer could think that papal infallibility could imply the pope could sing the song and be
guaranteed to have it be truth because of papal infallibility. This is falsehood. The doctrine of papal infallibility applies only to the religious doctrine of the Catholic
Church. Papal infallibility has only been invoked twice making the Immaculate Conception and Assumption of Mary timeless Catholic doctrine. The subject of the song does
not fall under possible timeless Catholic doctrine, as timeless Catholic doctrine cannot be made contradictory with itself. Anyone who claims papal infallibility is a loophole
for this song is simply incorrect.
6. The concept of Antipope gets closer to loophole territory. Historically there have been times when multiple humans claimed to be the Pope. The Vatican has an official list
of historical popes. The title of Antipope is bestowed on humans who had a legitimate claim to be Pope, but lost out in the end (the Vatican doesn't publish a list of Antipopes).
For an Antipope "I am the Pope" is claimed true at the time and "I'm not the Pope" is true in a historical sense. But both of those can't be true at the same point in time.
The only loophole here is if truth can exist in the presence of logical contradiction, which is not possible in human logic (past or present).
