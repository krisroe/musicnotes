If You are Fat then You are Not Elsie (Original Song)

If you are fat, then you are not Elsie.
If you're really fat, you still cannot be Elsie.
Only if you're super-duper lardballishly fat,
Can you come somewhat close to being Elsie.

Meaning: This song describes Elsie as fat in an over-the-top way. It defines three categories of fatness: "fat", "really fat", and "super-duper lardballishly fat". And even though the last category seems to be an over-the-top maximum category of fatness, it really isn't because Elsie is beyond that category as well. If you are in the last category you can only come "somewhat close" to Elsie's fatness. This isn't really an insult song, it's hyperbolizing Elsie's normal-scale-in-reality level of fatness into the impossible range.
