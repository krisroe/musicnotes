If I was an Omnipotent Being (Original Song)

Oh, if I was an omnipotent being,
What the heck would I do?
I'd sit around all day thinking
'Bout ways to torture you.

Meaning: This brief song (which is complete) was constructed when I was in college taking a philosophy class. Part of the philosophy class involved arguments involving the divine. At the time I was an atheist or agnostic, and this is a brief window into what I thought of God at the time. The song posits the singer is an omnipotent being (presumably a God). It euphemizes "hell" to "heck" which makes no sense because the God himself is singing the song and thus does not need to worry about blasphemy. It ponders what an omnipotent being would do, which is a very broad question given an omnipotent being can do anything (potentially subject to limits). The song answers this by saying God would sit around pondering (not doing much of anything). And maybe that makes logical sense if God is pondering the greater mysteries of creation that tax the capacity of an omnipotent being. But no, actually God sits around pondering the ways to torture the listener. God is thinking about things that are actively malicious to the lesser beings. Although God is just thinking about them, not actually doing them. Maybe God is pushing the boundaries of what a God is allowed to think, and maybe that actually helps God understand the smaller beings that do perform cruel or tortuous things.
