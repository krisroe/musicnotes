The Bird got out of its Cage (Partial Original)

The bird got out of its cage.
The cat tipped over the cage.
(Maybe) The house, it started on fire.

Meaning: When my mother was a young adult or possibly an older teenager, her family suffered a house fire. When my mother told the story she talked about her brother being in the garage with his motorcycle, and the behavior of the household pets. The story inspired me to make a brief song. The only two lines that I know for sure are the first two. I believe there may have been more talking about the fire more directly. The purpose of the song, from my (and possibly my sister's) point-of-view, was to irritate my mother. It may even be the first original song I made (certainly it fits with the theme of insulting/irritating songs that mark my first "compositions"). Sadly, it is not complete, and never will be.
