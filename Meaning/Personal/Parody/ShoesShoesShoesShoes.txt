Shoes Shoes Shoes Shoes (to the tune of Vengaboys Boom Boom Boom Boom refrain)

Shoes! Shoes! Shoes! Shoes!
I want to find my shoes
Let's find them both together
or have no shoes forever!

Shoes! Shoes! Shoes! Shoes!
I want to find them soon!
Or never will there ever
be shoes in any room!

Sung when looking for my shoes. "Let's find them both together" is a hope that the shoes will be in the same place, or I'll have to keep looking even after one is found. "or have no shoes forever" is an irrational worry that if I can't find my shoes, I'll never have any shoes ever again (notwithstanding that it would not be difficult to go to the store any but more). "I want to find them soon" indicates a sense of urgency, which can be particularly relevant if I have a time-sensitive task ahead. "Or never will there ever be shoes in any room" goes into the absurd, suggesting my loss of shoes somehow causes a global disappearance effect on the shoes of the world, and that loss cannot be remedied (notwithstanding that even if this happened it would not be difficult for shoes manufacturers to make more shoes). Fortunately, this is unnecessarily paranoid.
