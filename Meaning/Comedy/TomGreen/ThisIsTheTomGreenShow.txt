Meaning: This is the introduction song to Tom Green's MTV show. It doesn't describe much about the show other than it belongs to Tom Green and it is his favorite show. It explicitly points out that reversing his name is not what the song is. This is unnecessary since Green isn't really a first name. However "Green" could be used in this context as an adjective meaning inexperienced (Tom Green's humor was on the raw side of ridiculous) or money-grubbing (presumably Tom Green was paid for his antics).

The song also imagines the listener could have their own show. The song presumes that the listener would like their own show more than Tom Green would. Probably anyone capable of sustaining a real live show would like it because they would have some level of control over their song's content. But on some level that is absurd since most people couldn't be in a position to have a show of their own.
