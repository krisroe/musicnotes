Meaning: This is a song of seduction, with the singer trying to entice the listener into a romantic relationship. A sense of immediacy is presented (lets's start today and the time is right). The singer presents a worldview where the purposes of men and women are to be in relationships with each other. To bolster this thesis the Abrahamic creation story of Adam and Eve is brought up, where Eve was created as a counterpart to Adam. Romantic relationships, in particular the wooing parts, are described metaphorically as a game. The pet name "daddy" is imparted to the singer, who implores the listener to love him with all her might, and put arms around him, perhaps in a dance. The level of familiarity between the singer and listener is not given, and it could radically mean the song is too forward or over the top or regular romance. The game of love can have many outcomes if you play.
