Donald Trump is the 45th (elected 2016-11-08) and 47th (elected 2024-11-05) President of the United States. He won the 2016 election over Hillary Clinton while losing the popular vote. He lost the 2024 election to Joe Biden but has not publicly acknowledged this fact, instead claiming massive fraud. Usual election shenanigans occurred, such as misleading campaign advertisements. Covid (a vaccine was not widely available at this point) brought a new wrinkle into the voting process, where states changed election rules to make voting by mail easier to reduce Covid spread. Arguably some of these changes violated state law, but this was never vindicated in state courts. That doesn't cross the line into fraud, let alone massive fraud. From Donald Trump's public statements it seems he really believes there was massive fraud. It's hard to make reliable judgments about strangers' mental states, and though I can't definitively rule out feigned belief, I think the simplest explanation is the main psychological factor involved is delusion. I take the position that delusions (small and large) are a normal aspect of human psychology, and while it is unsettling that one of the world's most powerful politicians has a large and public delusion, I think that is the reality we live in.

The song was written shortly prior to the 2024 United States presidential election. The idea was winning the 2024 election when he had previously "died" in the 2020 election analogized to "rising" again. I was neutral between Joe Biden and Donald Trump and thought I could make a song that would work no matter who won. Does it cohere with the actual election results where Biden won but Trump claimed he actually won (rhetorical question)? Is the song work useful other elections where ideas related to Donald Trump are in play (another rhetorical question)?

The song draws connections between religion and politics. In society, both topics can incite strong beliefs, even to the point of refusing to acknowledge differing points of view (although most people I talk to are reasonable for both, I have a small circle of friends). The song analogizes Donald Trump (a highly controversial politician) with Jesus Christ (a human+divine figure in Christianity), even asking the question if he is God.

But politics can be taken too seriously, and two lines in the song suggest as such:
1. Election day is described as hallowed. Elections are routinely described as the most important election ever. Campaign ads regularly claim electing candidate such-and-such would produce catastrophic effects. In the United States, this has been the case for hundreds of years, but the world keeps on spinning.
2. Praying for a specific election outcome is mentioned. This isn't particularly notable, as people pray for a lot of different things. But it seems odd to me in a scientific world that God would actually intervene in an election.

The distinctive part of the song is a sequence of phrases, all ideas that could apply to Massive Fraud, with varying levels of truth and ridiculousness:
1. He defeated (e.g. in 2016 and 2024 Donald Trump won those elections despite Massive Fraud occurring in those elections too which seems absurd to me).
2. He outwitted (similar, but also referring to Donald Trump's cunning political strategy)
3. He conceited (used as an [archaic] verb this refers to forming an idea or imagining something, so this is perfectly reasonable to apply to Massive Fraud).
4. He concluded (something must be wrong with logical reasoning if this is the case)
5. He deluded (had a delusion, which I think is a reasonable word to apply to reality)
6. He extruded (we're past delusion into the realm of absurdity, meaning pushed or forced through a small opening)
7. He occluded (still in the realm of absurdity, meaning blocked, obstructed, or concealed from view or passage)
8. He denuded (still in the realm of absurdity, meaning deprived of power or authority)
9. He omitted (Donald Trump tailors his communication about the Massive Fraud and brings it up for audiences receptive to the idea but not other audiences)
10. He committed (Donald Trump has it backwards and he was the one committing Massive Fraud in the 2016 and 2024 elections)
11. HE DELUDED (In CAPS in the text version of the song, in voice should be sung significantly louder, to indicate this is the one the singer actually believes)

The song references "Banned from Twitter", a line which dates to events after the 2020 election. In the aftermath of the 2021-01-06 riot at the United States Capitol (which Donald Trump had a hand in inciting), Donald Trump was kicked off the Twitter social media platform on 2021-01-08. Donald Trump then found his own social media platform Truth Social which launched February 2022. Twitter was later acquired by Elon Musk (2022-10-27) and renamed X, but I didn't change the lyric of the song because "Twitter" fit better. Donald Trump's X account was reinstated in November 2022, but Truth Social remained his primary platform for posting his own messages.

The intent of the song is to have ambiguity for who the singer actually supports. "For this outcome we did pray" does not specify any particular election outcome. My claim is you could sing the song supporting Donald Trump or his ideas (despite the presence of psychological delusion) or opposing Donald Trump or his ideas (sung ironically). Or maybe this is too much ambiguity and I'm the only person in the universe who can truly appreciate this song.

The last line refers to Donald Trump pwning his enemies. Love him or hate him, there's definitely been some major pwnage.
