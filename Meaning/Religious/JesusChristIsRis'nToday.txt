Jesus Christ is Ris'n Today (Lyrical Adaptation of musicnotes.com MN0063770)

Jesus Christ is ris'n today. (Alleluia! follows each line)
Our triumphant holy day.
He died for us on the cross.
Suffered to redeem our loss.

Hymns of praise, it's time to sing.
Unto Christ, our beloved king.
He steadfastly bore the grave.
Sinners to forgive and save.

Thru the pains which He endured.
Our salvation is secured.
Now beyond the sky, divine.
Where souls and angels ever shine.

Our brave Lord, He reigns above.
Life eternal, as His love.
Praise Him, all ye heav'nly host.
Father, Son, and Holy Ghost.
