Zacchaeus was a Very Little Man (Children's Religious Song)

(Start Song)

Sung:
Zacchaeus was a very little man,
A very little man was he.
He climbed up in a sycamore tree,  [optional pantomime climbing a tree]
For the Lord he wanted to see.
And as the Savior passed that way,
He looked up in the tree.

Spoken:
He said "Zaccheus, you come down!" [optional pantomime cup hands around mouth]

Sung [optional clap to the beat]:
For I'm going to your house today!
For I'm going to your house today!

Meaning (also seen Luke 19:1-10): Zacchaeus was a wealthy tax collector from Jericho despised by the community. Zacchaeus had some kind of crisis of conscience and made a visit to see Jesus preach. His short stature and the large crowds induced him to climb a sycamore tree (Ficus sycomorus). When Jesus passed near the tree he recognized Zacchaeus and recognized he was near to conversion and public disapproval was a barrier. So Jesus gave Zacchaeus an invitation which was really more of a command. And that was just what was needed for Zacchaeus to publicly declare his change of faith.
